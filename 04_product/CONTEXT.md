# Product Context

## Purpose

Define what the team chooses to build based on the selected idea, problem, knowledge, and constraints.

Route here for requirements, features, future user flows, scope, and success criteria. Read the selected idea, relevant problem/knowledge, hackathon constraints, and current product artifacts first. Product choices may be modified here with rationale and traceability. Existing-world facts belong in `02_problem/` or `03_knowledge/`; technical design belongs in `05_architecture/`. Research unknown user or domain claims instead of converting guesses into requirements.

## Expected output

A coherent, testable product definition that architecture and build work can consume.
