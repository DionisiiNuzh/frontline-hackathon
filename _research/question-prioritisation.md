# Question Prioritisation

## Research question

How should the system determine which three unanswered questions would most improve the emergency response at any given moment?

## Unknowns to investigate

- Which operational outcomes should question priority optimise for?
- How should immediate life-safety risk, responder safety, location and access, required rescue capabilities, uncertainty reduction, and time sensitivity be weighted?
- Should a question rank higher when its answer could materially change which rescue team is recommended?
- Do real dispatch workflows or METHANE guidance define an accepted priority order, or is prioritisation dependent on incident type and organisational procedure?
- What information can callers reasonably and safely be expected to provide?

## Status

**OPEN:** No prioritisation model or weighting has been validated. Until research is completed, proposed ranking criteria should be treated as hypotheses rather than product requirements or domain knowledge.
