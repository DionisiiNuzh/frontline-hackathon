# Research Context

## Purpose

Discover or verify information needed by the project. This area is a pipeline, not the canonical knowledge store.

## Route here when

A concrete question cannot be answered from `_context/`, `02_problem/`, or `03_knowledge/`, or when an existing claim needs verification.

## Workflow

1. Add the question to `questions.md` and consult `research-guide.md`.
2. Put captured or lightly processed evidence in `raw/`, with provenance.
3. Put synthesized, qualified conclusions in `findings/`.
4. Validate the finding and update the appropriate canonical artifact when warranted.
5. Record unresolved uncertainty; do not silently promote it to fact.

Research may update questions, evidence, and findings. Durable domain/user understanding belongs in `03_knowledge/`; problem facts in `02_problem/`; event facts in `_context/`; decisions in product or architecture. Read only the materials relevant to the question.
