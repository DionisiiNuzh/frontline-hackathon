# Idea Context

## Purpose

Explore candidate solution ideas and preserve the selected concept before detailed product definition.

Route here for ideation, comparison, and concept selection. Read relevant hackathon constraints, problem context, and canonical knowledge first. Modify `ideas.md` during exploration and `final-idea.md` only when a concept is selected or revised. Do not put validated problem facts, detailed requirements, or architecture here. Use `_research/` when an idea relies on an unknown claim.

## Expected output

A traceable choice of concept and its rationale, ready to inform `04_product/`.
