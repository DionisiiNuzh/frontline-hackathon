# Minimal voice-run incident script

Record at normal conversational speed and leave a short pause between turns. Two speakers are preferable, but one person may read both roles. WAV is the preferred format; MP3 or M4A can also be used.

**Dispatcher:** Mountain Rescue. Tell me exactly what has happened.

**Caller:** My friend slipped from a ledge and fell below the path. He's awake and talking, but he says his left leg is badly hurt and he can't stand.

**Dispatcher:** Where are you now?

**Caller:** We're on North Ridge, close to the old quarry path. I don't know the grid reference. We walked up from the east car park.

**Dispatcher:** Are there any hazards around you?

**Caller:** The slope is very steep and there are loose rocks. It'll be dark in about an hour. I can see him, but I can't safely climb down to him.

**Dispatcher:** Are any emergency services already there?

**Caller:** No. It's only the two of us.
