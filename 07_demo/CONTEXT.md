# Demo Context

## Purpose

Shape the hackathon demonstration, pitch, submission, and judging narrative from verified product capabilities.

Route here for presentation and submission work. Read judging criteria, constraints, product scope/success criteria, and current working state first. Modify demo artifacts without overstating what works or inventing evidence. Product and architecture decisions remain in their own contexts; implementation remains in `app/`. Research any unknown submission or event requirement through `_research/`.

## Expected output

A consistent, truthful narrative and reproducible demo aligned with judging requirements.
