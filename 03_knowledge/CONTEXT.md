# Knowledge Context

## Purpose

Maintain the canonical, structured understanding of users and the domain for reliable retrieval by future agents.

Route here when reading or updating durable user/domain knowledge. Before modifying, inspect relevant findings and existing knowledge to avoid duplication. Claims should state scope, confidence or uncertainty, exceptions, applicability conditions, and provenance where useful.

Do not store chronological research notes or raw evidence here (`_research/`), product choices (`04_product/`), problem framing (`02_problem/`), or temporary status (`_state/`). If evidence is insufficient or conflicting, use `_research/` and leave the canonical claim qualified or unchanged.

## Expected output

Concise concept-oriented knowledge that downstream problem, product, and architecture work can safely cite.
