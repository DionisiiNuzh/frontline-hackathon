# Build Context

## Purpose

Coordinate implementation of approved product and architecture work. Application source lives in `app/`.

Route here when planning, sequencing, or tracking implementation. Read current state, scoped requirements, and relevant architecture first. Modify `tasks.md` and update `_state/CURRENT.md` after meaningful progress or blockers; modify `app/` only for an authorized implementation task. Do not redefine product requirements or architecture implicitly during coding—record such changes in their owning context. Use `_research/` for unresolved external or technical facts.

## Expected output

Small, verifiable tasks linked to requirements/design, plus accurate handoff state.
