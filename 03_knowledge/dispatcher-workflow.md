# Dispatcher Workflow

## Call handling and task delegation

**UNCERTAIN:** The dispatcher receives the original emergency call, speaks directly with the caller, gathers the incident information, and delegates the resulting task to a rescue team.

This is a working assumption for product design and has not yet been validated against a specific search-and-rescue organisation or operating model. Responsibilities may instead be divided between call handlers, dispatchers, control-room operators, or rescue coordinators.
